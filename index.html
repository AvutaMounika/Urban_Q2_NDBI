Interpretation of NDBI Results

Higher NDBI values: Indicate relatively greater built-up or bare surfaces. Lower NDBI values: Generally indicate vegetation, water or other surfaces with lower built-up characteristics. Temporal variation: Changes in mean NDBI between 2018 and 2025 reflect variations in surface characteristics across the study area. Seasonal influence: Differences in vegetation, crop conditions and surface moisture during January–March may influence annual NDBI values..
